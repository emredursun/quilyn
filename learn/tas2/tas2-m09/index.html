Grand Scenario: Order Your Own Computer

Objective

Independent hypothetical capstone: model configurable computer choices, expected prices and negative combinations in a data-driven Template. The following prices and restrictions are exercise assumptions, not verified current shop catalogue data or original Academy material.

Calculate expected values from the agreed hypothetical price specification, then compare the SUT output through appropriate Verify assertions. Computing the expectation in Tosca does not mean the SUT itself has calculated correctly.

FeatureOptions & Surcharge
Base packageCheap computer = $800.00 (standard) · Expensive computer = $1,800.00
ProcessorSlow = $0 · Medium = $15.00 (standard) · Fast = $100.00
RAM2 GB = $0 · 4 GB = $20.00 (standard) · 8 GB = $60.00
Hard Disk Drive320 GB = $0 (standard) · 400 GB = $100.00
SoftwareOffice Suite = standard · Image Viewer = +$5.00 · Office Suite (alt) = +$100.00 · Another Office Suite = +$40.00 (only ONE Office Suite allowed per order)
PaymentCOD (Cash on Delivery) only — remember its surcharge in the Total price calculation

Independent rule for this exercise: selecting both office suites is invalid. Verify the expected rejection, omit order completion and perform cleanup. Simply logging out without verifying rejection would not prove correct validation.

No original workspace subset is bundled or verified here. Inspect the actual Modules in your workspace; folder names and module availability are not guaranteed.
