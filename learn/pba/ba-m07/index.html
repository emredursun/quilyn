Pega Blueprint Overview

Pega Blueprint

A generative-AI-infused tool that changes how teams envision digital transformation. It enables rapid, collaborative application design through workflow design, helping teams align on an optimized solution and get into development far faster than the weeks-to-months traditional design once took.

Standalone SaaS tool

Blueprint operates INDEPENDENTLY as a SaaS tool, separate from existing Pega Studios — no Pega instance or login is required to collaborate (stakeholders with a Pega login can still participate). You create a Blueprint at pega.com/blueprint.

Augments, not replaces

Blueprint does NOT replace App Studio or other Pega tools; it augments them by offering a fresh, collaborative way to transform ideas into a functional application in record time, incorporating decades of Pega best practices through templates.

Blueprint is the architect's collaborative whiteboard: the whole team sketches the building together before any concrete is poured, using proven blueprints (best-practice templates), then hands the finished drawing to the construction crew (Pega Platform).

Generating a Pega Blueprint

Describe in business terms

You describe what you are trying to build in business terms; Blueprint uses that information to PROPOSE Case Types, Case Life Cycles, Data Models, data objects, and Personas — enabling quick design edits to fit business requirements.

Generate and modify

After the initial AI-proposed design, you refine it collaboratively — adding/editing Stages, Processes, Steps, fields, data objects, and Personas — so the Blueprint matches the desired workflow before importing.

Best-practice templates

Blueprint offers powerful, detailed best-practice application templates instantly, helping teams incorporate Pega's accumulated expertise rather than starting from a blank canvas.

Generating a Blueprint is like giving a skilled drafter a paragraph about your business: they hand back a labeled floor plan (Case Types, Life Cycles, Data Model, Personas) that you then mark up together until it's right.

Importing a Pega Blueprint into Pega Platform

Export and import

You export the resulting Blueprint and import the file directly into Pega Platform (a new or existing application), jumpstarting a demo or project build-out by producing a functional application in minutes.

Who imports it

The Lead System Architect (LSA) is responsible for importing the Blueprint file into the Pega Platform environment and uses the user stories created by Blueprint and BAs to configure functional and technical requirements.

What import creates (and the exception)

On import, Pega creates the Blueprint's assets according to your configuration. ALL the assets are created in a Branch EXCEPT Access Roles.

PremBridge not required

Pega GenAI PremBridge is NOT required to import Pega Blueprints into a new or existing application.

Importing a Blueprint is like importing a fully-furnished room layout into your house plan: everything drops into a staging area (a Branch) for review — except the keys (Access Roles), which are handled separately.

Using Blueprint for an Industry App (Customer Service for Insurance)

Industry templates

Blueprint can be used to create industry-specific applications — for example, a Customer Service for Insurance application — by starting from relevant best-practice templates and refining the proposed Case Types and workflows for the industry's needs.

Industry templates are pre-fab housing kits: instead of designing an insurance service app from scratch, you start from a kit tuned for that industry and customize the rooms (Case Types/workflows).
