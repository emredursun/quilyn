Beyond a single run: the Insights dashboard

TestOps Dashboard

Insights provides branch-scoped Dashboard and Reports views with available plan features. Use their statistics to investigate test quality/activity; they do not alone certify overall application health or requirement coverage.

Metrics that matter beyond pass/fail

Compare metrics under the same relevant selection and time scope. Correlated pass-rate, duration or activity changes can guide investigation but do not prove the cause; lower activity does not by itself establish shrinking test coverage.

The Dashboard's panes, in detail

Assigned to me leaves Duplication Level project-wide, while the remote pane switches from executions to owned-test runs. The remote pane time/filter selection does not change Test Overview or Duplication Level; do not compare these panes as though they share one denominator.
