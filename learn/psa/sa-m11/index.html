Case Status

Case status

Case status communicates progress. It uses Work-.pyStatusWork; configured Rules and notifications may refer to it, but the value does not by itself guarantee a particular notification.

Case status and assignment instructions are like a traffic light and GPS directions combined: the status indicator tells everyone where the case is in its journey (Open / Pending-Approval / Resolved), while the step instructions are turn-by-turn directions telling the current worker exactly what to do before pressing 'Submit.' Miss either one and the driver is left guessing at a green light.

Status categories

Four conventional Case status prefix categories:

  • New — just created
  • Open — in progress
  • Pending — waiting on something or someone
  • Resolved — processing is complete, with no remaining open Assignments; examples include Resolved-Completed and Resolved-Rejected

Setting status

Configure status at meaningful Stages or Steps. A configured transition updates status; the final Stage’s resolution status applies after its Processes complete. Not every transition needs a different status.

Guiding users: instructions, help text, and visual cues

Step (Assignment) instructions

Text added to a Collect-information Step/Assignment that tells the user exactly what they must do to complete the Task. Instructions render at runtime on the assignment form, reducing confusion and errors.

Contextual field text

Additional text beside an input can clarify the expected value. Instruction text explains the task; neither replaces validation or authorization.

Instruction overrides

The Form can show the configured Step instructions, override them with task-specific text, or hide them. Choose guidance that helps the user complete the current Assignment.
