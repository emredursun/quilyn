Cascading approvals: what and when

Cascading approval

Approvals routed SEQUENTIALLY — one approver at a time. The next approver is asked only AFTER the current one approves. Not a parallel vote or majority tally — each level must approve in turn.

Cascading approvals are like a chain of waterfalls: the case flows from one level of authority to the next, each approver reviewing and passing it downstream. Configure the chain with an authority matrix that maps reporting relationships — like water that must pass through every fall in sequence before reaching the pool at the bottom, with no fall skippable.

Single vs. cascading

Two approval routing modes:

  • SINGLE — exactly one approver; configure the Approval Step with a specific approver
  • CASCADING — chains multiple approvals together; levels configured by an authority matrix or When rules

Two models for the approver chain

REPORTING STRUCTURE — climbs the org chart from submitter upward (manager, manager's manager, etc.). AUTHORITY MATRIX — a decision table lists the specific approvers and conditions, including people OUTSIDE the reporting line.

Four design questions

Answer before configuring: (1) Who approves? (2) How many approvals? (3) Is the entire reporting hierarchy required or only a subset? (4) Are approvers outside the hierarchy needed? Answers 3–4 determine reporting structure vs. authority matrix.

Rejection ends the chain

A REJECTION at any level stops the cascade immediately — remaining approvers are never asked, and the Case routes to the configured rejection/rework path. Every approver in the chain must approve for the Case to pass.

Reporting structure (and conditional levels)

Reporting structure approval

Configured in Dev Studio: add an Approve/Reject Step, select Cascading → Reporting structure. The approver comes from the operator's ID record (reporting or workgroup manager), and the cascade climbs the org chart automatically.

Number of approvers: One / All / Custom

Reporting structure offers three climb settings: ONE (immediate manager only), ALL (full hierarchy to the top), or CUSTOM (specific levels driven by When rules, e.g., small expenses stop at manager; large ones cascade further).

Authority matrix

Authority matrix approval

Select Cascading → Authority matrix in Dev Studio. The approver list is populated from a Decision Table, Data Page, Activity, or Data Transform. Each satisfied row adds its approver; Assignments route in list order.

Decision table: set 'Evaluate all rows'

CRITICAL: an authority matrix Decision table must be set to EVALUATE ALL ROWS — otherwise it stops at the FIRST matching row, returns only ONE approver, and the cascade is incomplete.

When authority matrix beats reporting structure

Use authority matrix when approvals must include people OUTSIDE the reporting structure — e.g., Legal, Compliance, Procurement. More flexible than reporting structure, which can only reach people in the management hierarchy.
