Data encryption

Choose the right mechanism

Encryption protects sensitive persisted data using managed cryptographic keys. Individual properties and BLOB data can have different encryption configurations; consider exposed columns as well as serialized data. Password hashing is a different, irreversible mechanism. Encryption does not replace authorization or safe key handling.

Worked example: Protect a sensitive account field and review its exposed-column storage as well as the case BLOB. Manage keys securely and verify that only authorized paths can obtain readable data.

Field-level auditing

Choose the right mechanism

Standard case history records actions; field-level auditing records configured field changes, including old/new values, actor and time. Enable auditing for relevant fields and inspect embedded data fields when needed. Auditing is evidence after a change, not a mechanism that blocks the change.

Worked example: Audit a dispatch address so investigators can see who changed it and when. Keep access to the audit record appropriate because it can contain previous sensitive values.
